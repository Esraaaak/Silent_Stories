Antonio Canova

Italy | Neoclassical Sculptor

About the Artist

Antonio Canova (1757-1822) was an Italian Neoclassical sculptor famous for his marble sculptures that exemplify idealized beauty, harmony, and classical grace. His works often depicted mythological subjects with meticulous attention to detail.
