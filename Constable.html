John Constable

England | Romantic Landscape Painter

About the Artist

John Constable (1776-1837) was an English Romantic painter famous for his landscape paintings of the English countryside. His works are celebrated for their naturalistic depiction of skies, clouds, and rural scenes, emphasizing light and atmosphere.
