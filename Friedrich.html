Caspar David Friedrich

Germany | Romantic Painter

About the Artist

Caspar David Friedrich (1774-1840) was a German Romantic painter best known for his landscapes imbued with a sense of melancholy and spirituality. He often depicted solitary figures amidst vast natural settings to evoke contemplation and the sublime.
